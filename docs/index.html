The useful part is the thinking

A character needs
something to do.

01 / Notice

Read the mark.

Keep a recognisable shape, colour relationship, or peculiar little detail. The brand is the starting material.

02 / Translate

Give it a job.

Four fragments can be a team. Many publishing routes can become many busy arms. Find the ability in the idea.

03 / Play

Let it go a little wrong.

A researcher tangled in relationships. A bird tripping over its own frames. A small consequence makes a story.

04 / Finish

Keep the paper real.

Matte printed colour, a thin white cut edge, a clear silhouette, and actual transparent space around the sticker.
