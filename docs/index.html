More than a face on a logo

A character.
Something to do.

The skill starts with the shapes and ideas already in your brand. Those become a cast, an ability, or a way of moving.

Then comes the story: an action, a small mishap, a reaction. Keep the same character across the set, and finish each scene with a real paper-sticker edge.

Make your own

Your logo.
A whole new cast.

Bring a mark you care about.
Give your agent the rest.

01

Add the skill

Install it in your agent with the Skills CLI.

npx skills add huyanxius/logo-to-stickers

02

Bring your logo

Attach the actual mark, then ask your agent:

Use $logo-to-stickers to make five playful paper stickers from this logo. Keep the brand recognisable, give each image a little story, and save five separate transparent PNGs.

03

Meet your characters

Review the first sticker, use it as a reference for the rest, and export your set as transparent PNGs.

Read the full guide

Tested with Codex’s built-in image generator. Your agent needs a reference-capable image tool with transparent output; the skill provides the workflow, not an image model.
