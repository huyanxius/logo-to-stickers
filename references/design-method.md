# Three real translations

These examples came from one portfolio session on October 15–16, 2026. The initial attempts were too literal and stiff. The useful change was to make the brand's shapes or behaviour participate in a small story. All three directions shown here were subsequently accepted by the person commissioning the stickers.

## Qunxue: four fragments become a research team

Inspect the [original mark](../docs/assets/logo-qunxue.png) and the [fieldwork sticker](../docs/assets/qunxue-01.webp).

The mark contains four asymmetrical charcoal fragments. Each becomes a body with tiny ivory eyes and ink-line limbs. The distinct shapes, count, and charcoal/ivory relationship carry the identity. Small ochre and dusty terracotta props support the scenes.

The team inspects a daisy through a giant magnifying glass, builds a wobbly ladder, gets tangled in a relationship thread, conducts a paper-cup interview, and hides in a book. Their collaboration is the source of personality. The enlarged eye in the first image belongs to the lens joke; later scenes return to ordinary dot eyes.

## MultiPush: publishing in many directions becomes many busy arms

Inspect the [original mark](../docs/assets/logo-multipush.png) and the [postal writer](../docs/assets/multipush-01.webp).

The project was previously named MultiPublish. Its mark has blue outward arrows, a green upward arrow, and a white ring on black. The octopus is a new metaphor developed for this session, not a pre-existing official mascot.

Cobalt blue, mint undersides, expressive eyes, cheek freckles, and a charcoal bag with the small arrow mark define the character. Ink and gouache give it a little volume while retaining a matte printed surface. Its many arms type, fold, carry, tangle, and finally relax. The useful ability creates both competence and comic trouble.

## Windup: the actual mechanical bird comes to life

Inspect the [original mark](../docs/assets/logo-windup.png) and the [overwound bird](../docs/assets/windup-01.webp).

An earlier attempt centred on a witch character and did not work for this set. The accepted revision returned to the mechanical-bird logo: plump charcoal body, rounded head, split pointed tail, and an inset wing with a round hinge and two diagonal strokes.

The wing becomes an ivory paper panel. An ochre winding key gives the bird a small mechanical ability. It winds itself too far, trips over its own animation strip, draws an impractically large wing, steps out of a storyboard frame, and sleeps in a paper-strip nest. Animation is present in the action itself.

## What travels to the next brand

The repeatable method is the relationship between the mark, the character, its ability, and the consequence of an action. The four paper researchers, octopus, bird, palettes, and props remain specific to these examples.

Display images are compressed copies of the generated originals. The [run records](../examples/prompts.json) include all fifteen stories, prompts, and ordered reference roles. Use the prompt template for new work rather than copying historical reference numbering without checking it.
